Research

EUSIPCO 2026, accepted

Beyond the Seen: Environmental Sound Deepfake Detection Across Unseen Generators

K. Naduvathra Revi, M. Bhattacharya, S. A. Shaik Adam

Our entry to the ICASSP 2026 Grand Challenge (Track 1). EAT+AASIST pairs a pre-trained Efficient Audio Transformer with a graph-based AASIST backend. Differential fine-tuning and data augmentation (codec simulation, gain normalisation) help it generalise to generators it never saw in training.

2.48% EER on EnvSDD, an 81% relative improvement over the strongest baseline (13.20%).
